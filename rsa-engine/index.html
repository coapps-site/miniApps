Simple Span — panel/post spans floor-to-ceiling (Hr) with no intermediate support; both ends free to rotate. Current default, and the basis for all 18 checks as originally implemented.
Continuous — panel/post continues over an intermediate support (e.g. a mezzanine or mid-height girt tied into structure); lower moment and deflection than simple span for the same total height.
Cantilever — fixed at the base, free at the top (e.g. parapet or partial-height wall); higher moment, deflection and buckling demand than simple span for the same height.
Braced — simple-span behaviour but at a shorter effective span, set by an intermediate horizontal girt/purlin at the spacing given below (rather than the full Hr).

6. Mullion Post, Truss & Fastener Mechanics

Trusses span the room's shorter side and repeat at Struss centres along the longer side, with a minimum of 2 trusses regardless of spacing. For a square room (Lr = Wr) the automatic rule is ambiguous — set Truss Span Direction explicitly. Truss spans of 12.0m or more require top suspended support (Check 18); below 12.0m no top suspended support is required. Base Anchor Spacing sa is the centre-to-centre spacing of anchors along the base track on a straight run — typically 4–5m, independent of Post Spacing Spost — and sets the tributary width for Check 15's per-anchor shear demand. Top track anchors (typically at ~1.2m centres) are not currently a separate check.
